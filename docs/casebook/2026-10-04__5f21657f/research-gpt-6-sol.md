# Independent research report: delegate model choice

Model ID: **gpt-6-sol**. Research date: **2026-10-04**.

## Read this first

The best default for a substantial delegated coding or research task is **gpt-6.1-sol at medium or high effort**, with **claude-opus-5-5 at medium or high** as the strongest broadly useful alternative. That is my inference, not a universal benchmark result. Artificial Analysis measured GPT-6.1 Sol near Astra's quality at much lower task cost, while Opus 5.5 led its agentic knowledge work and aggregate index. For a truly hard novel reasoning task, compare **gpt-6-astra** and **claude-opus-5-5**, then try **claude-fable-5-1** if the Anthropic path fails. The lead can change by task: Epoch found Astra ahead on math and Fable 5.1 ahead on software engineering, with overlapping confidence intervals. [Independent, Artificial Analysis, 2026-09-29](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence/), [independent, Artificial Analysis, 2026-09-22](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-opus-5-5), [independent, Epoch AI, 2026-09-16](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/astra-eci-breakdown).

This report distinguishes **source measurements** from **my selection inferences**. Artificial Analysis (AA) supplies most cross-model cost and agent evidence here. Its many pages are one evaluator and **not corroborating independent studies**. Epoch AI and Scale Labs add different tests, but they do not provide comparable end-to-end costs for all 15 models. AA's dollar figure is an average cost per *attempted Intelligence Index task*, not cost per successful real delegate assignment. Its Coding Agent Index includes model plus harness and can better represent terminal execution, but it still does not isolate the model from the harness. [Independent, AA methodology, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking), [independent, AA coding agent analysis, 2026-09-09](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/benchmarking-gpt-6-astra), [independent, Epoch AI, 2026-09-16](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/astra-eci-breakdown).

## Cross-checks and limits that affect choice

* **Depth differs by domain.** Epoch's September 16 composite put Astra at 166, Fable 5.1 at 164, and GPT-5.6 Sol at 162. Its math subset favored Astra, its software engineering subset favored Fable 5.1, and its 90% confidence intervals overlapped. Scale Labs' HLE Diamond page placed Astra at 60.6%, Opus 5.5 at 55.0%, Fable 5.1 at 51.3%, but this is a closed-book knowledge and reasoning test, not a coding agent run. [Independent Epoch, 2026-09-16](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/astra-eci-breakdown), [independent Scale Labs live leaderboard, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/hle-diamond).
* **Execution results are conditional on harness and effort.** AA's September 9 coding agent test put Astra and Fable 5.1 level at 62, with Astra using far fewer tokens and costing about 40% less per task. AA's September 29 test found GPT-6.1 Sol xhigh one point above Astra at under 15% of its cost per coding task, while 6.1 Sol max scored three points below its own xhigh setting. That reversal is a warning against equating maximum effort with best execution. AA's September 28 Terminal-Bench 4.0 run found Sonnet 5.5 max at 64%, Opus 5.5 and Astra at about 60%, with Sonnet using about seven times Astra's output tokens per Intelligence Index task. All three claims come from **the same AA evaluator**, not three corroborating studies. [Independent AA, 2026-09-09](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/benchmarking-gpt-6-astra), [independent AA, 2026-09-29](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence/), [independent AA, 2026-09-28](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-sonnet-5-5).
* **Care with other software tests.** Scale Labs' live SWE Atlas page showed Astra and Fable 5.1 close on refactoring, Fable 5.1 ahead of older Fable and Opus 5 on test writing, and Opus 5 ahead on codebase Q&A. This gives task-specific hints, but model and harness differ and intervals are broad. Epoch now marks DeepSWE v1.1 flawed. I would avoid making DeepSWE alone a route rule, even though it appears inside larger indices. [Independent Scale Labs live leaderboard, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard), [independent Epoch live review, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/deepswe).
* **Task costs can reverse token-price intuition.** AA measured Astra at about one third the output tokens of Fable 5.1 at the same index score, and Sonnet 5.5 at max at the highest token use it had measured. A cheap rate with many extra steps, output tokens, or failed attempts can lose. Neither AA nor the vendor pages measures this repo's exact delegate workloads, so actual cost per completed assignment remains unknown. [Independent AA, 2026-09-09](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/benchmarking-gpt-6-astra), [independent AA, 2026-09-28](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-sonnet-5-5), [vendor Anthropic cost guidance, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/optimizing-for-cost-and-intelligence).

## Effort, price, and routing recommendation

**Measured:** both vendors sell higher reasoning effort at the same per-token rate but the model usually emits more reasoning/output tokens and takes longer. AA measured GPT-6.1 Sol from $0.21 per index task at medium to $0.72 at max, with index score rising from 48 to 52. Sonnet 5.5 rose from index 36 at low to 56 at max, but max reached roughly $7.6 per task. Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 have always-on adaptive thinking, with effort controlling depth. Sonnet 5.5 has adaptive thinking and can suppress up-front thinking at some settings. GPT-6.1 Sol does not support `none` or `minimal`. GPT-6 Sol and Luna do. [Independent AA Sol comparison, undated live snapshot, accessed 2026-10-04](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/releases/comparisons/gpt-6-1-sol-vs-gpt-6-sol), [independent AA Sonnet, 2026-09-28](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-sonnet-5-5), [vendor Anthropic model pages, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview), [vendor OpenAI model pages, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models).

**My inference and recommended decision guide:**

1. For bounded mechanical work with an easy verifier, begin with `gpt-6-luna` at low or medium. Use `claude-haiku-4-5` if Claude behavior matters. If failures force expensive retries, move to `gpt-6.1-sol` rather than assuming the cheapest token rate remains cheapest per completion. The large Luna to Sol terminal performance gap in AA makes this escalation important. [Independent AA Luna comparison, undated live snapshot, accessed 2026-10-04](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/comparisons/claude-sonnet-5-5-high-vs-gpt-6-luna), [independent AA Sol, 2026-09-29](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence/).
2. For a well-specified multi-file implementation or long terminal loop, start with `gpt-6.1-sol` medium. Try high or xhigh when the first pass stalls. Test `claude-sonnet-5-5` high or `claude-opus-5-5` medium for tasks needing Claude-style execution. Avoid making Sonnet max the default solely from its top terminal score, because its token and task cost expand sharply there. [Independent AA, 2026-09-29](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence/), [independent AA, 2026-09-28](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-sonnet-5-5), [independent AA, 2026-09-22](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-opus-5-5).
3. For an underspecified design, scientific or mathematical problem, or difficult codebase diagnosis, compare `gpt-6-astra` high or xhigh with `claude-opus-5-5` high. For especially stubborn code or long research, try `claude-fable-5-1` high. Select by the resulting answer and a task-specific verifier rather than declaring one model the global depth winner. This follows Epoch's domain split, AA's Opus agentic knowledge lead, and Scale's differing software results. [Independent Epoch, 2026-09-16](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/astra-eci-breakdown), [independent AA, 2026-09-22](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-opus-5-5), [independent Scale Labs live leaderboard, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard).
4. Keep the older listed models as a compact compatibility section. Current vendor catalogs foreground Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 4.5 and Astra, 6.1 Sol, 6 Luna. Anthropic explicitly marks Fable 5, Opus 5, Opus 4.8, and Sonnet 5 as active legacy. I found no evidence that any requested ID is retired or renamed. `gpt-6-sol` remains documented but is no longer OpenAI's featured Sol model. [Vendor Anthropic model pages, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview), [vendor OpenAI catalog, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models), [vendor OpenAI GPT-6 Sol page, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-sol).

**Suggested reference layout:** a short task-to-model decision guide first, then a table with separate columns for **depth**, **long-loop execution**, **measured cost per attempted task**, **confidence**, **effort sweet spot**, and **status**. Place prices and cache rates in a dated appendix, not as the main cost ranking. Show the evaluator, harness, effort, and test date next to any numerical score. Put legacy models below current defaults. Add a sentence that actual cost per completed delegate assignment depends on success and retry rate, which the available cross-model data does not supply. This structure is my recommendation based on the evidence above.

## Missing models and unresolved signal

Anthropic also lists `claude-mythos-5-1` and `claude-mythos-5`, but both are **invite only** Project Glasswing models. Anthropic says Mythos 5.1 shares Fable 5.1's specifications and pricing, so I would mention it as an availability exception rather than a normal delegate choice. OpenAI lists specialized models including GPT-5.6 Cyber, but its public general-purpose flagship lineup is Astra, 6.1 Sol, and Luna. I found no other generally available Anthropic or OpenAI GPT model that should displace a requested ID in this general-purpose delegation guide. [Vendor Anthropic Mythos 5.1 page, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/mythos-5-1/overview), [vendor Anthropic Mythos 5 page, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/mythos-5/overview), [vendor OpenAI catalog, undated, accessed 2026-10-04](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models).

The largest missing signal is a same-task, same-harness, repeated **cost per successful completion** comparison of 6.1 Sol, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Astra, Fable 5.1, and the small models on this repository's actual delegate work. I did not run that evaluation. I also found little independent evidence for 6.1 Sol outside AA because it launched only September 29. These gaps should temper numerical routing rules.
